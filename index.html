Your first chord shapes

Practical framing: a chord shape isn't magic, it's just a handful of notes from a scale, stacked so they ring together. Knowing the notes makes the shape make sense.

Chord switching, in time

The thing that actually slows beginners down isn't the shapes, it's switching between them at tempo. Pick a progression, set a slow BPM, and stay locked to the click even if the chord you land on is sloppy at first.
